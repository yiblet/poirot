# Boost.Math binary64 K approximations

The engineering module adapts the K0/K1 binary64 bases from Boost.Math 1.85.0,
revision `434a017db4c7abc115d8a8ae08a446b70feb34f2`, under BSL-1.0.
The exact source URLs, original copyright notices, adaptation scope and hashes
are in [SPECIAL_SOURCES.md](https://github.com/yiblet/poirot/blob/master/crates/poirot-functions/src/engineering/SPECIAL_SOURCES.md).
The complete [Boost license](https://github.com/yiblet/poirot/blob/master/crates/poirot-functions/src/engineering/BOOST_LICENSE_1_0.txt)
is packaged with that source. No other Boost implementation, test or data file
was reused. Poirot's MIT license does not replace these retained obligations.
